Suggestions

Pizzas people built in the app and sent as ideas for the menu. “Add to menu” opens a new pizza already filled in; nothing goes on the menu until you save it.

      Toppings

      Tap a square to add or change its photo (shown in the app). Photos are cropped square and shrunk to 256 px here before upload; a close-up on a plain background looks best. The drop-down sets how the app draws it on the pizza picture. ◀▶ set the order the app lists them in.
      Pieces: for a topping the app has no pieces for, add a pieces sheet: one picture with a few separate pieces of it (slices, leaves, chunks) on a transparent or plain white / light-grey background, with a gap around each. It is cut into single pieces here, and the app scatters those on its pizza.

        Sauces

        The colour is what the app paints on the pizza picture. Each pizza has a default sauce; people can swap it.
